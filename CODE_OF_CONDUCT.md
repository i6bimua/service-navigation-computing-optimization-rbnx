# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus feedback on technical work;
- welcome users with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- document benchmark conditions and limitations honestly;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private
  information;
- respect model, dataset, simulator, and software licenses.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory
language, deliberate misrepresentation of results, publishing credentials or
private data, and sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `3087918372@qq.com`. Maintainers may edit
or remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern is not permitted.
